Malachy asked for the mechanism behind 32,877 detections / ~1,265 per scene. It is not tuning and
it is not a bad threshold value. robust_threshold (optical.py:629-654) sets thr = median + N*sigma
with sigma = MAD_TO_SIGMA * MAD, and MAD_TO_SIGMA = 1.4826 is the GAUSSIAN consistency constant --
it converts MAD to sigma only if the data are Gaussian. Ocean NIR is not, and not by a little.

MEASURED on 20200730 (the only scene on the hub), 10.83 M water px through the real water mask,
median 0.01740, MAD-sigma 0.003855:

     4     0.03282      37,171       3.43e-03        3.17e-05         108x
     5     0.03667      19,971       1.84e-03        2.87e-07       6,424x

At the N=6 this batch ran, a Gaussian predicts 0.01 PIXELS IN THE WHOLE SCENE. There are 14,128.

THE TAIL BARELY DECAYS, WHICH IS THE PART THAT MATTERS. 6 sigma -> 10 sigma only HALVES the count
(14,128 -> 7,450); a Gaussian would drop it fourteen orders of magnitude. So N is very nearly a
dead knob: the detector's only tuning parameter is close to disconnected from its output. That is
the mechanism behind Malachy's "the mass piles up at the cut with no natural break", and it means
the section 8 N-sweep as currently framed CANNOT converge on a clean value, because there is no
value to find. Sweeping N harder is not the fix.

MY SELF-TEST DID NOT CATCH THIS AND COULD NOT HAVE. My "N=6 removes all of them while still

scripts/optical_selftest.py. Synthetic noise is Gaussian, so the fixture satisfies exactly the
assumption the ocean breaks. 44/44 still passes and the code is still correct against its spec;
the spec is what is wrong. Recording this as the general lesson: a synthetic fixture validates
the implementation, never the distributional assumption underneath it. Invariant 9's cousin.

min_px = 2 IS WHAT TURNS THAT TAIL INTO "VESSELS". BLOB_MIN_PIXELS = 2 (optical.py:268). Of the
32,877 rows: 46% are EXACTLY 2 px (15,269), 72% are <= 4 px. At 3 m GSD a 2 px blob is 6.0 m,
which is both the minimum length in the file and its p25. Two adjacent pixels among ~14,000
scattered over 10.8 M is a coincidence that happens constantly. A 2 px blob also has no shape, so
its minAreaRect length and aspect are artefacts -- hence the spikes at exactly 6.0 m and aspect
2.0. Across the 26 scenes this is measurably the driver: r = +0.86 between log10(n_detections)
and the fraction of blobs <= 4 px.

WHAT I EXPECTED AND WAS WRONG ABOUT: SUN GLINT. glint_angle vs log10(n_detections) is r = +0.12
across the 26 scenes -- no relationship. sun_elevation is -0.25. Posting the negative result so
nobody re-runs it. Counts swing 258x (32 to 8,257) over the same 100 km2 with every scene >= 98%
clear, so something scene-state dependent drives it, but the acquisition-geometry columns in the
manifest do not capture it. Consistent reading: the threshold is SCENE-RELATIVE, MAD measures the
water's core texture, and the heavy tail does not scale with it -- so on a scene whose core is
quiet, sigma shrinks, the threshold slides toward the median, and the tail floods through.
Sensitivity is set by sea-surface texture, not by anything about boats. Supporting: r = -0.56 for
median nir_snr, and r = -0.82 for the fraction of detections in multi-year cells, i.e. the runaway
scenes are NOT rocks, they are fresh per-scene noise.

THE STRUCTURAL PART, AND IT IS BIGGER THAN A TUNING PROBLEM. A 6 m boat is 2 px at PlanetScope
3 m; a 12 m boat is ~4 px. The FAO 0-12 m class -- the class goal 2 and the no-AIS framing care
MOST about -- sits at or below the resolution limit. So min_px cannot simply be raised to kill the
noise without deleting the target class, and no shape test (aspect, compactness, orientation) is
available at 2-4 px either. This deserves a decision record and a sentence in section 13: for the
smallest FAO class, PlanetScope may support DETECTION-BY-PERSISTENCE-ABSENCE but not per-scene
per-object detection. That is a claim about the sensor, not about our code.

WHAT SURVIVES, AND WHAT I SUGGEST. The persistence filter is the one thing here that is
principled -- it makes no distributional assumption at all, which is exactly why it is the piece
worth building on. Two concrete suggestions, neither implemented, both Malachy's call since it is
his script: (1) replace the sigma-multiple threshold with an EMPIRICAL-QUANTILE threshold
