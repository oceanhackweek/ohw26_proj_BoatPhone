- THE MODEL CHANGED. We are no longer buying VTUAD or training our own classifier. Ocean Networks
  Canada has released a pretrained model (github.com/OceanNetworksCanada/selfsupervision_anomalies_onc,
  MIT) with an "Engine Noise" class, trained on ONC hydrophone spectrograms -- and OUR device,
  ICLISTENHF1266, is in its training/eval set. It consumes ONC spectrogram arrays, so our .fft.gz
  product is the native input; no WAV pull needed. Decision 0009.
- Why VTUAD was dropped: no vessel size or length label (so the size-stratified transfer gap the
  old plan promised cannot be computed), uncalibrated audio against our calibrated data, and a paid
  IEEE DataPort subscription with no download API. Also established: NO public AIS-trained acoustic
  model weights exist anywhere -- we verified Domingos (code, no weights), Decrop et al. (dataset,
  no weights) and Renaud et al. (method only). Facts in docs/vtuad-facts.md and references/.
- Band reframing that matters for expectations: the >=250 Hz floor is NOT a problem for the small
  recreational craft we care about. Small planing hulls peak around 1-10 kHz; it is LARGE ships
  whose diagnostic tonals sit at 10-100 Hz. The floor costs us large-vessel detection. Decision 0010.
- Next: B0, a half-day gate confirming ONC's model actually accepts our .fft.gz surface. If it does
  not, we fall back to standard band-level SPL detection (PyPAM/pbp/MHKiT), which needs no model.
