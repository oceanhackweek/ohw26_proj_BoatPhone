  recreational craft we care about. Small planing hulls peak around 1-10 kHz; it is LARGE ships
  whose diagnostic tonals sit at 10-100 Hz. The floor costs us large-vessel detection. Decision 0010.

Project Goals:

1) Create a model that uses hydrophone data and prior knowledge of vessels detected from optical satellite imagery to create continuous estimates of the number of boats, 
2) Distinguish between larger or smaller boats (those without AIS) 
    - The UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) primarily classifies fishing vessel lengths overall (LOA) 
    - Three core size categories for statistical and safety frameworks: 0–12 meters, 12–24 meters, and above 24 meters.
3) Identify the useful range of acoustic detection of ships
4) Focused on Bamfield Area using the CIOOS data from F____ hydrophone pair
4) We are specifically interested in small / recreational ships that do not have AIS
4) Can we identify speed? Changes in pitch/frequency
